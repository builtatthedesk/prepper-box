Sources

  • FCC rules: 47 CFR Parts 97, 95 and 80, Electronic Code of Federal Regulations, ecfr.gov (fetched at build time).
  • NOAA Weather Radio transmitters: National Weather Service station data, weather.gov/nwr (fetched at build time).
  • Calling and simplex frequencies: ARRL band plan, arrl.org/band-plan (Nov 2026). Facts only; ARRL text not reproduced.
  • Phonetic alphabet: NATO, nato.int.
  • Q-codes: ITU-R Recommendation M.1172, "Miscellaneous abbreviations and signals to be used for radiocommunications in the maritime mobile service".
  • RST system: as recommended by the ARRL.
  • Net procedure and message format: ARRL Public Service Communications Manual, Appendix B, NTS Methods and Practices Guidelines, chapters 1 and 4 (summarised).

Band plans and local repeater coordination change. When internet is back, recheck arrl.org/band-plan, your regional repeater coordinator and the FCC rules.
