Marine VHF channels

From 47 CFR § 80.373(f) (eCFR text below, with its footnotes). Marine VHF is FM, 156–162 MHz. A ship station that doesn't go to foreign ports or make international calls, and isn't required to carry a radio, is licensed by rule and needs no individual FCC licence (§ 80.13(c)). Marine channels are for ships and licensed coast stations, not general use from land.
