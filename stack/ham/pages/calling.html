Calling and simplex frequencies

From the ARRL band plan (arrl.org/band-plan, read Nov 2026). Band plans are voluntary agreements, not FCC rules; your class's privileges are on the band page. Your area's repeater and simplex plan is set by the regional frequency coordinator and can differ from the national plan away from the national calling frequencies, so listen before you transmit.
