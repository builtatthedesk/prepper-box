Rules that apply everywhere

  • CW is allowed on any frequency your license class may use (§ 97.305(a)).
  • Maximum power is 1.5 kW PEP, and you must use the minimum power needed (§ 97.313(a), (b)).
  • Technician HF: the 80, 40 and 15 m segments are CW only; on 10 m, phone is SSB only, 28.3–28.5 (§ 97.307(f)(9), (10)).
  • RTTY/data on HF is limited to 2.8 kHz bandwidth (§ 97.307(f)(3)).
  • Give your call sign at the end of each contact and at least every 10 minutes during it (§ 97.119).
  • Novice licences are no longer issued. Novices have the Technician HF segments above, plus 222–225 MHz (25 W) and 1270–1295 MHz (5 W).
